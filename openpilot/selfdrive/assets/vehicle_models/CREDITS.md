# Native driving display vehicle artwork

`road_vehicles.png` contains three pre-rendered views of each of the five meshes below. The renders use the project's decimated, reoriented meshes in `openpilot/selfdrive/carrot/cluster/assets/models` with new lighting, paint and transparent backgrounds. The first row is the project's original generic passenger car. It represents other traffic without assigning a make or model to a radar return.

- [Hyundai Ioniq 5 - Lowpoly](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hyundai-ioniq-5-lowpoly-675e78311e8440d88714bd212cb7a8fb) by andikapratamaw.
