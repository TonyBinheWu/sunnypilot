## Automatic turn signals

`AutoBlinkerControl`

0 Bluetooth one-touch. Mode 1 can hold a Bluetooth signal started at or below 5 km/h until the turn completes. Mode 2 also supports explicit driver-requested maneuvers. The same button cancels, the opposite button switches direction and the stock lever takes priority. Route cues use the display and audio.
