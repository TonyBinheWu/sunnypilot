<!-- CARROT:SECTION:BEGIN BEHAVIOR -->
### Behavior

Controls the volume multiplier for engage, disengage, and reverse prompts.

- **Higher:** Makes those state prompts louder.
- **Lower:** Makes those state prompts quieter.
<!-- CARROT:SECTION:END BEHAVIOR -->

<!-- CARROT:SECTION:BEGIN USAGE -->
### Usage

While parked, play prompts and verify language, general volume, and engage/disengage volume separately.
